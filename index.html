Hi! I'm Alexa. I cover AI hardware, robotics, semiconductors, and healthtech on a lean global tech equities team at BlackRock. I graduated from Harvard in 2025, where I studied Neuroscience and English. My research background is in the neuroscience of language: I completed a computational psycholinguistics study at UC Santa Barbara — acknowledged in Entropy — and trained in MIT's EvLab (Fedorenko Lab), doing fMRI training and contributing to experimental design. My coursework spans systems neuroscience, neural computation, cell biology, and the science of consciousness. My laboratory training includes hands-on work in molecular biology, cell culture, and biochemical assays, alongside computational research. Lately I've been tinkering with a few projects on GitHub.

I maintain two Substacks — Walking Into the Light, on coexisting with machine intelligence, and Walking Out of the Light, on consciousness — and I've honed my editorial craft as editor-in-chief of IMAGINATION and on staff at COUNTERCLOCK. My poetry has appeared in The Offing, DIALOGIST, and Lunch Ticket, and was recognized with Harvard's Edward Eager Memorial Prize. I'm a voracious reader with a soft spot for psychoanalysis and lyrical poetry (lately, Michel Serres). Add me on Goodreads!

I'm a longtime sci-fi and anime fan (Attack on Titan above all) and can usually be found at a convention or two each year.
